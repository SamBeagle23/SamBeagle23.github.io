Work outside the studio: a charity market, a streetwear brand and film photography.

Charity vintage market creation

As a sophomore at Georgia Tech, I noticed that vintage clothing pop-up markets were all around Atlanta, but not yet on campus. I worked with local vendors to start Tech’s first one: Sigma Nu Secondhand. I also create all the digital and print advertising for the events.

The vendors’ payment goes completely towards charity. We’ve raised over $15,000 for the American Foundation for Suicide Prevention as of today!
